# Showing Up Club

A calming, romantic, and low-friction web application designed to track your daily prescribed physiotherapy routine and nurture a lasting habit of care.

---

### Key Features

1. **"A reminder to myself" (Daily Affirmations)**
   - Displayed prominently on the home screen.
   - Rotates daily based on the calendar date to remain an anchor for the day.
   - Tap **"A little reminder"** anytime you need extra grounded encouragement.
   - All 15 personal affirmations centered on small habits, consistency, future self, and playing your violin again.

2. **"My Exercises" (Prescribed Strengthening Checklist)**
   - **Chin tuck** (2 × 10)
   - **Butterfly scapula** (2 × 10)
   - **Neck up and down while maintaining a neutral position** (1 × 10)
   - **Right and left neck movement, holding for one second** (1 × 10)
   - **Lateral bend** (1 × 10)
   - **Interactive Sets**: Track Set 1 and Set 2 separately for 2-set exercises, or check off the entire exercise with a single click.
   - **Cadence & 1-Second Hold Pacer**: Includes a minimalist breathing/movement pacer designed specifically for exercises requiring holds.
   - **Daily Completion Summary**: Live progress counter (e.g., *"3 of 5 exercises completed"*), progress bar, and percentage circle.
   - **Satisfying Completion**: When all 5 are completed, a gentle harmonic singing-bowl chime and subtle pastel particle bloom celebrate your 5 minutes of care:
     > *"That was five minutes you chose for yourself. Keep showing up, one day at a time."*
   - **Instant Undo**: Easily undo any accidental tap.

3. **Romantic Calligraphy Fonts & Cute Color Themes**
   - Romantic cursive script headers inspired by Parisienne & Didone calligraphy styles.
   - 6 cute pastel and cozy dark themes (🌸 Sakura, 🍵 Matcha, 🍯 Honey, 🫐 Lavender, 🌙 Midnight, 🍫 Cocoa).
   - 1-click Dark/Light mode toggle.

4. **Dedicated 4-Hour Relief Session Log**
   - Kept strictly separate from the daily strengthening routine.
   - Log quick posture checks, heat packs, or gentle pauses throughout the day.

5. **Physiotherapy Settings**
   - Editable exercise names, sets, and repetitions in Settings if your physiotherapist adjusts your routine.
   - One-click button to reset back to the default prescribed routine.

---

### Running the Application

npm run dev
